# Design System

## Register
product

## Visual Theme & Palette
- Background: Deep Obsidian `#090d16`
- Surface: Glass Dark Slate `rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.85)`
- Border: Translucent border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
- Primary Accent: Indigo Glow `#6366f1` / `#818cf8`
- PDF Action Accent: Sky Blue `#38bdf8` / `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`
- Success / Complete: Emerald `#10b981`
- Text Ink: Pure White `#f8fafc`, Muted `#94a3b8`, Dim `#64748b`

## Typography
- System Sans Stack: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", "Microsoft YaHei", sans-serif`
- Tabular Numbers for Time & Sizes: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`

## Breakpoints
- Desktop: `> 880px` (Dual column layout)
- Mobile: `<= 880px` (Single column with compact mini-player bar and expandable modal, full screen PDF in-app viewer)
